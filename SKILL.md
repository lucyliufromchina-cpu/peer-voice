# Peer-Voice / 平视写作

## The one idea / 一句话内核

**EN —** Condescension (爹味) means your text positions you *above* the reader —
judging them, diagnosing their failures, or teaching them their own business. The
fix is to level with them: hand the **problem** to industry / structure / the natural
stage of things, hand the **judgment** back to the reader, and keep for yourself only
what you can honestly own — what you **see** and what you **do**.

**中文 —** 爹味 = 文字把自己摆在读者之上(评判他 / 诊断他失败 / 教他做他自己的事)。
去爹味 = 平视:把"问题"归给行业和结构,把"判断"还给读者,自己只保留"我看见什么、我做什么"。

The hidden sentence behind every condescending line is **"I understand your business
better than you do."** Rewrite it into:

> **"You know your business best; I only know the one thing in my hands — so let's
> look at it side by side."**
> 每一句爹味的潜台词都是"我比你更懂你的事";改写目标是"你的事你最懂,我只懂手里这点,我们平起平坐一起看"。

## The five moves / 五个动作

A condescending line is almost always breaking one of these five. Find which one and apply the matching move.

### 1. Attribute outward, never to the reader's negligence / 归因外部,不归客户失职
Don't say "nobody does X" or "you have a blind spot" (= you failed). Say it's the
industry's next step, a structural fact, or a stage everyone passes through. Same gap
named, but no blame — so the reader leans in instead of getting defensive.
归因给行业 / 结构 / 演进,而不是客户的疏忽。同样指出缺口,但听者不觉得被指责。
- ❌ 一直没人系统地做过 / 没人对过账 / 你有个盲区
- ✅ 这是整个行业才刚开始做的一层 / 不在谁没做,而在数据天生是分开的

### 2. Leave the judgment with the reader / 判断留给客户
State facts and ask questions; do not prescribe decisions, teach them how to run their
business, or assign them org roles. Their business is theirs to decide.
只摆事实、提问题;不替对方下决定、不教他经营、不替他派角色。
- ❌ 这件事为什么由你 IT 牵头最合适 / 你该把这家店关掉
- ✅ 我们想和 IT 一起做什么 / 做不做、怎么做,你定

### 3. Speak upward, not downward / 向上的语言
Frame value as lifting quality, not as patching the reader's leaks. "More consistent →
easier for everyone," not "you're losing money / you're exposed."
把价值说成"把质量往上提",不是"补你的漏洞 / 你在漏钱 / 你的风险敞口"。
- ❌ 法务暴露 + 财务漏损
- ✅ 口径越统一,法务和财务越省心

### 4. Earn trust by your track record, not by belittling them / 立信靠资历,不靠贬低对方
Build credibility with what *you've* done, explicitly credit what the reader already
does well, then narrow to one humble opening. Never open by flexing your own trophies.
用"我们干这行最久 / 做过多少家"立信;先给对方戴高帽(承认他强的地方),再把切口缩到一个谦虚的小范围。开场别晒自己的成绩单。
- ❌ 你这套系统用得不错(给甲方打分) / 开场先列我们做了 A、做了 B
- ✅ 相信贵司在 X 上已经很成熟;这里只是值得单独看一眼的一小块

### 5. Change the subject from "you" to "we / the industry / the thing" / 主语从"你"换成"我们 / 行业 / 事情"
The fastest tell of condescension is a sentence whose subject is "you [lack / should /
are wrong]". Re-subject it to "we see / we do / this is hard because…".
爹味最快的判据,是句子主语为"你(没 / 该 / 错)"。把主语换成"我们看到 / 我们做 / 这件事难在…"。
- ❌ 你没有把…串起来 / 你应该…
- ✅ 我们看到的一件事是… / 我们做的就是…

## How to apply / 怎么用

1. **Scan** the draft for the trigger phrases in `references/trigger-words.md`. Each hit is a red flag.
   先用 `references/trigger-words.md` 的触发词表扫一遍,命中即红旗。
2. **Diagnose** which "above-the-reader" stance each red flag takes (one of the five moves).
   判断每个红旗踩了五个动作里的哪一条。
3. **Rewrite** with the matching move. Worked before→after rewrites are in `references/examples.md`.
   用对应动作改写;改写范例见 `references/examples.md`。
4. **Re-check** every sentence with the ruler below.
   用下面这把尺子逐句复查。

## The one-line self-check / 一句自检尺子

> For each sentence ask: **is the subject "you" or "we"?**
> If it grades, diagnoses, or instructs the reader — rewrite it.
> If it only says what **we** see, do, or understand — keep it.
> 每句话问一遍:**主语是"你"还是"我们"?** 评判 / 诊断 / 教导对方的,改;只讲"我们看见 / 我们做 / 我们理解"的,留。

## Also note / 附带一条

Condescension and verbosity share a root: over-explaining assumes the reader won't get
it; self-promotional preamble decides your value *for* them. Cutting the
"thinking-for-them" lines usually makes the copy shorter too — humbler and tighter
arrive together.
爹味和啰嗦同根:过度解释=假设你不懂;自夸铺垫=替你判断我的价值。删掉"替你想 / 替你夸"的话,字数自己就下来了——更谦逊和更简洁会一起到。

## Scope / 适用范围

Tuned for Chinese B2B / vendor-to-client copy, where the native word for this failure
is 爹味. The **principle** (level with the reader) is universal; the **trigger list and
examples** are Chinese-specific. For other languages, keep the five moves and the
self-check ruler; rebuild the trigger list in that language.
为中文 B2B / 乙方对甲方文案调校:内核普适,触发词表与例子针对中文。换语言时,五个动作和自检尺子照用,触发词表按那门语言重建。
